CapCut AI Tutorial: Make Professional Videos in 15 Minutes with Zero Experience

CapCut Video Tutorial Zero Experience

Why CapCut AI

Short video is the biggest traffic channel in 2026 — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels dominate attention. But traditional video production requires learning editing, voiceover, subtitling, and asset sourcing. CapCut AI automates all of these. If you can type, you can make videos.

Prerequisites

  • Download CapCut (desktop recommended for efficiency; also available on mobile)
  • Create an account (sign in with TikTok/Google)
  • Prepare a text script (300-800 words for a 1-3 minute video)

No script? Use ChatGPT to generate one for free: "Write a 60-second short video script about 3 AI tools that boost productivity. Structure: hook, 3 tool intros, CTA."

Step 1: AI Text-to-Video (5 min)

  1. Open CapCut desktop, click "AI Text to Video" on the home screen
  2. Paste your text script into the input box
  3. Choose video style: Knowledge/Education (recommended), Emotional Story, or Product Showcase
  4. Select AI voice: "Knowledge Male" (authoritative) or "Warm Female" (friendly)
  5. Click "Generate" and wait 2-5 minutes

You will receive a complete video with AI-matched stock footage + AI voiceover + subtitles + transitions. 90% of the time this version is ready to publish.

Step 2: Footage Fine-Tuning (3 min)

If some AI-matched clips are unsatisfactory:

  1. Click the segment on the timeline you want to replace
  2. Search the stock library on the right (keywords: "tech," "office," "AI," "abstract")
  3. Select a better clip and drag to replace
  4. The library includes 2M+ free assets covering virtually every scenario

Step 3: Smart Subtitles (1 min)

  1. Click "Subtitles" in the top menu, then "Auto Subtitles"
  2. Select language (English/Chinese/Bilingual)
  3. Wait for AI recognition (~10 seconds per minute of video)
  4. Manually edit any errors after recognition
  5. Choose subtitle style: position, font size, color

Step 4: Add Background Music (1 min)

  1. Click "Audio" then "Music Library"
  2. Search by mood keyword: "upbeat," "tech," "inspiring," "chill"
  3. Select a track and drag to the timeline
  4. Critical: Set BGM volume to 15%-20% so it doesn't overpower the voiceover

Step 5: Thumbnail & Export (2 min)

  1. Scrub to the most visually striking frame, click "Set as Thumbnail"
  2. Add text title on the thumbnail (large font, high-contrast color)
  3. Choose export format: 1080P (recommended balance of quality and file size)
  4. Click "Export" and wait for rendering
  5. Share directly to YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels

Office Automation Make Efficiency

What Is Office Automation

The core idea of office automation is simple: when A happens, automatically do B. For example: when an email attachment arrives, auto-save it to cloud storage; when a new order comes in, auto-notify the team; when you finish an article, auto-distribute it to multiple platforms. In 2026, with tools like Make and Zapier, all of this requires zero coding.

5 Most Practical Automation Scenarios

Scenario 1: Content Auto-Distribution (Essential for Creators)

Pain point: After writing an article, manually copying to Medium, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Discord — 20-30 minutes each time.

Automation plan:

  1. Write article in Notion, mark status as "Ready to Publish"
  2. Make detects status change, triggers workflow
  3. Text Parser extracts title, body, and tags
  4. Auto-publish to Twitter (direct API)
  5. Auto-publish to LinkedIn (API)
  6. Auto-publish to Medium (API)
  7. Auto-post to Discord webhook

Result: 30 articles/month saves 10-15 hours, zero misses.

Scenario 2: Email Attachment Auto-Filing

Pain point: Receiving many emails daily, manually downloading, renaming, and categorizing attachments.

Automation plan:

  1. Trigger: New email received
  2. Filter: Only process emails with attachments
  3. Action 1: Download attachment to Google Drive / Dropbox folder
  4. Action 2: Rename to "Sender + Date + Filename" format
  5. Action 3: Create a record in Notion with file link

Scenario 3: Automated Weekly Report Generation

Pain point: Manually exporting data from multiple systems every week to compile a report.

Automation plan:

  1. Scheduled trigger: Every Monday at 9 AM
  2. Action 1: Pull data from Google Sheets / database
  3. Action 2: Use DeepSeek API to analyze trends
  4. Action 3: Use Notion AI to generate the analysis report
  5. Action 4: Send to Slack/Discord channel

Scenario 4: Auto-Response to Customer Inquiries

Pain point: Customer inquiries need fast response, but 24/7 manual monitoring is impractical.

Automation plan:

  1. Trigger: Customer email / form submission received
  2. Action 1: Use ChatGPT/DeepSeek API to analyze inquiry content
  3. Action 2: Generate personalized response (based on preset templates)
  4. Action 3: Send auto-reply email
  5. Action 4: Create follow-up record in CRM/Notion
  6. Action 5: Notify sales team

Scenario 5: Scheduled Social Media Publishing

Pain point: Content needs to post at optimal times, but you often forget or are unavailable.

Automation plan:

  1. Use ChatGPT to batch-generate a week's content
  2. Create a content calendar database in Notion
  3. Make checks on schedule, auto-publishes when time arrives
  4. Auto-post to Twitter, LinkedIn, Medium
  5. After publishing, auto-collect engagement metrics (views, likes, comments)
